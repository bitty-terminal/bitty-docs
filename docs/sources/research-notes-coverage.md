# Research notes coverage

The workspace keeps a numbered research-record series in the `research`
repository
([bitty-terminal/research](https://github.com/bitty-terminal/research)):
immutable originals under `origin/NNN.md` and trimmed English summaries under
`summary/NNN.md`, `001` through `055` (records `048`-`054` landed via research push `dd4cfd3`, record `055` via push `e7a18d3`).
A record is marked complete by renaming
its original to `origin/NNN.md.completed` and flipping the summary Status to
`Captured`, meaning its conclusions should have landed in the `*-docs`
repositories. The records are durable scratch input, not canonical documents:
they are not copied into this repository, they are not implementation evidence,
and nothing in them is normative unless a canonical document establishes it.
This page is the traceability record from each record to the documents that
carry its substance.

Completion marking is uneven (2026-09-16 whole-workspace review, report 03).
This ledger therefore states only the capture it can verify and labels the
rest:

- **Recorded** — the linked canonical documents exist and carry the direction;
  shared-governance and terminal-corpus links were verified when the row was
  last updated.
- **Owner-confirmed** — the destination owner confirmed the state in the
  2026-09-16 review: record 039's terminal direction is captured, and records
  042/043 are captured in `bitty-docs`. Records 032/038 are
  captured-candidate plugin-side directions (see rows 032/038), not omissions.
- **Needs owner confirmation** — `bitty-ai-docs` or `bitty-plugins-docs` owns
  the destination; the research summary marks the record `Captured`, but this
  ledger does not assert capture for a corpus it does not own.
- **Open** — the research summary states conclusions are not yet captured in any
  `*-docs` corpus (records 046/047/053/054/055 at the time of writing);
  destinations below are proposed routing only, never capture claims.
- **Partial (bitty-ai distillation)** — records 048-052: the remote summary
  marks the `bitty-ai`-owned conclusions as distilled into draft discussion
  syntheses in `bitty-ai-docs` (048/049 via
  `research-distillation-048-049-bitty-ai.md` in PR
  [bitty-ai-docs#120](https://github.com/bitty-terminal/bitty-ai-docs/pull/120),
  050/051 via `research-distillation-050-051-bitty-ai.md` in the same PR,
  052 via `research-distillation-052-bitty-ai.md` in PR
  [bitty-ai-docs#122](https://github.com/bitty-terminal/bitty-ai-docs/pull/122));
  captures in other corpora (Wheel/harness, context, tool/ACI, skill,
  verification, plugin-ecosystem owners) are not verified and stay
  owner-pending. The origins stay unrenamed.

## Interpretation rules

The same rules as the [shared-conversation
coverage](chatgpt-share-coverage.md) apply. A research record is historical
design input and provenance. When a record and the maintained corpus disagree,
the corpus wins and the record stays as evidence of the earlier direction. The
`research` archive is untrusted, read-only research material: the canonical
documents linked below are the authority, and a corpus owner's capture claim
outranks this ledger's reading of the archive. Rows for records `001`-`016`
keep the deposit evidence recorded when they were written and were not
re-audited by the 2026-09-16 refresh; the labels above apply to the
`017`-`043` rows, where the audit found the completion marking uneven.
Rows `044`-`054` were added 2026-09-17 under CTX-0223: the `044`/`045`
bitty-side captures were verified merged on `main` (PR #321/#323) while
`bitty-ai` and harness sides stay owner-pending; `046`/`047`/`053`/`054`
are Open with proposed routing only. Rows `048`-`055` were refreshed
2026-09-18 under CTX-0228 to the verified post-publication state: `048`-`052`
are Partial per their remote summaries (`bitty-ai` distillations via
bitty-ai-docs PR #120/#122, remaining owners pending; research pushes
`dd4cfd3`/`e7a18d3` landed on research `main`), `053`/`054`/`055` are Open
with published remote summaries (research README rows confirm the
Partial/Open split).
